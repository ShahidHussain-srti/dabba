One thin divider wherever two compartments' areas meet, as thick as the inner-wall setting. Knuckles alternate between base and lid on one axis. Push a length of 1.75 mm filament through the hole, then melt or trim the ends. The 2.1 mm hole leaves room for holes printed on their side coming out a little small; go to 2.2 mm if the pin still binds, or 2.0 mm for a stiffer hinge.

Clasps

A flexible tab on the lid hooks into a slot in the base. Prints upright with the lid, no supports; flick the grip to open.

A separate hook pivots on a filament pin through lugs on the lid, and swings down over a second filament pin held by lugs on the base. Print the hook on its side.

A jewellery-box swing hook: a flat hook turns on a filament pin set into a boss on the lid, and its curved tip threads through an eye on the base. Turn the knob to open. The hook prints flat as its own piece.

A stiff tab hangs from the lid on a thin web near its top. Press the tab above the web and it rocks, lifting its hook out of the slot in the base; close the lid and it clicks back in. The web is both hinge and spring, and it prints flat, so it bends along its layers rather than across them.

Pockets for round magnets in the base rim and lid, meeting face to face. Glue them in, minding the polarity.

Small ridges on the alignment lip click into matching dimples in the lid. Needs the lip.
